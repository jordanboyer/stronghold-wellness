What It Is

The StrongHold Wellness Portal is a mobile-optimized fitness experience accessible to every French Quarter Inn guest via QR code in their room. Guests get three complimentary guided sessions — morning mobility, full-body strength, and bedtime recovery — and can unlock four premium workouts for the duration of their stay with a single $29 purchase.

Check-In Talking Point

"We've partnered with StrongHold Wellness to bring expert-guided fitness sessions right to your room. You've got three complimentary workouts — just scan the QR code on your welcome card or visit the link in your key packet. If you'd like the full library, it's $29 to unlock everything for your stay."

Keep it brief. If the guest looks interested, add: "It works great right in the room — no equipment needed for the free sessions, and the recovery session is a nice wind-down after a full day in the French Quarter."

Common Guest Questions
Do guests need to create an account?
No. Just scan the QR code and go. Entering an email is optional — it lets them save progress across devices.
What's the $29 charge for?
It unlocks all four premium sessions for the full stay. One-time charge, processed securely through Stripe.
Does access expire at checkout?
Yes. Guests receive a follow-up email with post-stay program options from strongholdfitness.co.
Something isn't loading or working?
Direct the guest to the URL below or contact Jordan directly. Most issues resolve with a browser refresh.
Is the Front Desk available to assist guests?
Yes. Any Front Desk staff can walk a guest through the QR scan. It works on any smartphone browser — no app download needed.
